# KAKE design system

Private, mobile-first routine and food log for the author's school, training and meals.

## Visual contract
- Background: `#0c1118` (ink blue-black); surface: `#141d2a`; raised surface: `#111821`.
- Primary action: `#3b82f6`; pressed/active: `#2563eb`; luminous detail/focus: `#60a5fa`.
- Text: `#edf3fb`; secondary: `#90a0b5`; divider: `#263243`.
- Typography: DM Sans for all interface content, with system sans fallback; comfortable compact scale and sentence-case labels.
- Layout: left-aligned phone-first timeline; energy record and water shortcut lead the Today screen; persistent bottom navigation for six destinations.
- Signature detail: the water bottle quick action and school-day meal timeline give the home screen its personal rhythm.
- Surfaces use restrained blue-black fills and thin blue-gray borders. Blue is reserved for action, focus and progress; no gradients or generic icon-card hero.
- Motion only confirms user actions; respect reduced motion and visible keyboard focus.

## Pages
Today, Food, Movement, Evolution, Assessment and Routine share this token, type, navigation and spacing contract.
